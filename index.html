06 · Marathon postmortem

Four builds → one PR pattern

Toggle races to compare Strava-synced 12-week windows. Eugene broke through despite never hitting 50 mpw and zero midweek MLRs.

What worked · Eugene

  • Faster aerobic baseline (median easy 8:15 vs 8:42 / 9:00 prior)
  • Shamrock HM PR as fitness check ~6 weeks out
  • Two continuous 20 mi longs at ~8:04–8:14 — not 22+ slogs
  • Cleaner late taper (final build week 60% of peak)
  • Stacked marathon experience / durability

What didn't

  • Zero midweek MLR ≥10 across all four builds
  • Never reached 50 mpw — Tucson 50–55 still uncharted
  • Boom-bust weeks (stdev ~9–11 mpw)
  • Debut 22.4 mi long did not help (4:27)
  • Gray-zone easies (fast pace + HR 155–158)
  • Post-tune-up recovery cliffs (Eugene W09 = 10.1 mpw)

Tucson opportunities

  • Add Wed MLR 10–14 to hit 50–55 without Sunday >20
  • Easy discipline: 8:45–9:30 conversational (not 8:15 gray zone)
  • Downhill / eccentric prep for net downhill course
  • Fuel longs/MP at 60–90 g CHO/h — gut train
  • Race-day shoe + long-run shoe continuity
07 · Coach rules

What Potato enforces

Non-negotiables from the postmortem + COACH_LOOP. Red days are a veto, not a suggestion.

BAN

Week-to-week volume jump >25–30%

BAN

Sunday long >20 mi — extend volume via midweek MLR first

ENFORCE

Easy pace floor: warn if non-quality runs avg faster than ~8:40 with HR ≥155

REQUIRE

≥1 named quality (T or MP continuous) most weeks in specific/peak

GATE

Red Oura / niggle: no MP or intervals

CUTBACK

Every 3rd–4th week 10–20% volume

AFTER

Tune-up race: planned rebuild week — not accidental 10 mpw hole

FUEL

Mandate on all ≥90 min / any MP block: 60–90 g CHO/h
